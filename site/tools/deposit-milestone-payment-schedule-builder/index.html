Tips

  • Tie each milestone to something the client can see and approve, not hours worked.
  • Keep the final payment smaller than the deposit where you can: it's the one most likely to slip.
  • Release final files once the final payment clears.
  • Amounts are rounded to whole units; any rounding difference goes on the final payment so the total always matches.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
