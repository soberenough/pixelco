How to use the number

  1. Open a separate account (or pot) for the business, one for tax, and pay everything that comes in there first.
  2. On the same day each month, move the steady paycheck to your personal account. Do not pay yourself more in a good month.
  3. Whatever is left above the paycheck builds the buffer. Once the buffer reaches your target, you can raise the paycheck a little, or put the extra toward other goals.
  4. If the buffer ever drops below one month of essential costs, cut the paycheck to essentials only until it recovers, and put your energy into finding work.
  5. Run this again every quarter with fresh numbers.

This is a planning aid that does simple arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It is general information, not financial or tax advice. The right tax set-aside depends on where you live and how your business is set up, so confirm it with an accountant or your tax authority's guidance.
