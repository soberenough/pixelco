How to read this

  • Top client share is the percent of your income from your biggest client. Many freelancers aim to keep it under about 30-40%; above 50% you're closer to an employee without the protections.
  • Top 3 share shows whether a few clients carry everything. Above 80% with only a handful of clients is fragile.
  • Effective number of clients answers: "my income is as spread out as if I had N equal clients." Five clients where one pays 70% behave like fewer than two.
  • Lose-a-client test: the monthly gap if that client stopped tomorrow, and whether your savings cover it for the time it takes to replace them.
  • These thresholds are rules of thumb, not financial advice. A big, stable, long-contract client can be fine; a small, unpredictable one can still hurt.
