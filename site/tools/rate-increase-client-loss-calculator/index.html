Rate Increase Calculator: How Many Clients Can You Afford to Lose?

Thinking about raising your rates but worried some clients will leave? Enter your current clients and the increase you have in mind. You will see what you earn if everyone stays, exactly how much work you could lose and still be ahead, what happens if each client walks away, and a notice email for each client with their new rate and start date. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Your increase

Give clients at least 30 days' notice, and 60-90 days for retainer or long-standing clients. The tool warns you if the start date is closer than that.

2. Your clients

Use an average month for hours. Project-priced client? Divide a typical monthly invoice by the hours it takes and enter that as the hourly rate. "Own %" overrides the increase for one client: use 0 for a client you are deliberately not raising this time.
