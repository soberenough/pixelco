# PixelCo short-video scripts: 4-week test (12 reels)

**Goal:** comments → DMs → free download → $7 Get-Paid Kit sales. One keyword everywhere: **PAID**.
**Format:** 15–30 s, vertical 1080×1920, no face, no camera. Either a **screen recording** of a kit file being filled in, or **text on screen** over a plain brand background (ink #1C1E26, orange #E85D3A, cream #FAF6EE). Optional AI voiceover; if used, turn on the platform's AI label and add "AI voiceover" to the caption.
**Rules:** no income claims, no invented testimonials, show the real product, captions on every clip.
**Post:** Reels + TikTok + Shorts, Mon/Wed/Fri. Track comments, DMs, free downloads and kit sales each week.

The DM the automation sends (Meta "Comment to message", keyword PAID):
> Here are the 3 late-payment emails, free: [Gumroad $0 link]. Copy, fill in the brackets, send. If you want the final-notice email, payment terms and the invoice tracker too, the full kit is $7.

---

## Week 1: the problem

**1. "The invoice nobody answered"** (text on screen, 20 s)
- 0–3 s: "You sent the invoice 3 weeks ago."
- 3–7 s: "No reply. No payment."
- 7–14 s: "Most late invoices aren't refused. The email just got buried."
- 14–20 s: "Send this the day after it's due →" (show email 1 subject line)
- CTA: "Comment PAID and I'll DM you all 3 follow-up emails, free."
- Caption: The day-after email that gets most invoices paid. Comment PAID for all three.

**2. "Don't say 'just checking in'"** (text on screen, 15 s)
- ❌ "Just checking in on that invoice!"
- ✅ "Invoice #104 for $850 was due on the 3rd. I've attached it again."
- "Specific beats polite. Every time."
- CTA: "Comment PAID for the exact wording."

**3. "Watch me fill it in"** (screen recording, 25 s)
- Open the Payment Reminders doc, fill [name], [#], [amount], [date] in real time.
- On-screen text: "30 seconds. No awkward rewriting."
- CTA: "Comment PAID, I'll send you the template."

## Week 2: the sequence

**4. "Day 1, Day 7, Day 30"** (text on screen, 25 s)
- "Late-payment emails, in order:"
- "Day 1: friendly reminder, invoice attached."
- "Day 7: 'Is something blocking this? I'll fix it today.'"
- "Day 30: 'Please confirm a payment date.'"
- CTA: "Comment PAID for all three, ready to copy."

**5. "The question that unsticks payment"** (text on screen, 15 s)
- "Ask: 'Does this need a PO or approval on your side?'"
- "Half the time, it's sitting in someone's queue."
- CTA: "Comment PAID for the full email."

**6. "Tuesday morning rule"** (text on screen, 15 s)
- "Send payment reminders Tuesday–Thursday mornings."
- "Friday 5 PM emails get buried until Monday."
- CTA: "Comment PAID for the reminders."

## Week 3: prevent it

**7. "Put this in every contract"** (screen recording of Payment Terms doc, 25 s)
- Highlight: deposit, due date, late fee line.
- "Late fees only work if you agreed them up front."
- CTA: "Comment PAID for the free reminder emails; payment terms are in the full kit."

**8. "Ask for a deposit"** (text on screen, 20 s)
- "New client? Ask for 30–50% up front."
- "Good clients don't blink. Risky ones tell you something."
- CTA: "Comment PAID."

**9. "Invoice checklist in 10 seconds"** (screen recording, 15 s)
- Scroll the Invoice Checklist: PO number, correct contact, due date, payment details.
- "Most 'late' invoices were missing one of these."
- CTA: "Comment PAID."

## Week 4: track it

**10. "Where's every invoice?"** (screen recording of Invoice Tracker spreadsheet, 25 s)
- Show status column turning red when overdue.
- "If you can't see it, you can't chase it."
- CTA: "Comment PAID for the free emails; the tracker is in the $7 kit."

**11. "Stay factual"** (text on screen, 15 s)
- ❌ "I'm really disappointed…"
- ✅ "Invoice #104 is 30 days overdue. Please confirm a payment date."
- "Facts get paid. Feelings get ignored."
- CTA: "Comment PAID."

**12. "The whole system, 30 seconds"** (mixed, 30 s)
- Clip montage: terms → checklist → invoice → reminders → tracker.
- "Get paid on time without awkward chasing."
- CTA: "Comment PAID for the free emails. Full kit $7, link in bio."

## Go / no-go after 4 weeks (suggested)
- Keep going if: ≥1 kit sale per 100 free downloads, or comments and downloads clearly trending up.
- Stop or change format if: <20 comments total across 12 reels.
