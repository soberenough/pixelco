## Headline (recommended)
**STOP WRITING "JUST CHECKING IN." / MAKE AI WRITE YOUR PAYMENT REMINDERS.**

I chose this over "Stop paying for invoice-chasing apps" for three reasons:
- **The app version overclaims.** Apps send reminders automatically and track payments, and a prompt doesn't. It's also a little awkward when we're selling a $7 kit ourselves.
- **"Just checking in" is the exact pain** every freelancer recognizes.
- **It says "AI" rather than "Claude."** The prompt works anywhere, and that avoids implying any tie to Anthropic. The caption can still say "works in Claude, ChatGPT or any AI chat."
