How to use this well: only charge a late fee your client agreed to in writing (in the contract, proposal or invoice terms) before the work began. Many places limit late fees or interest on business debts, and some set a statutory rate you may claim instead, so check the rules where you and your client are based. This tool is general information, not legal advice. A polite reminder sent on the first day overdue gets most invoices paid; fees work best as a clear, pre-agreed consequence rather than a surprise.
